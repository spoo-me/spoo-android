The official spoo.me app: shorten, manage, and analyze your links from your pocket, built end to end in Material 3 Expressive.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Share to shorten.</b> Send any link from any app's share sheet and it lands in the create sheet, ready to go.</li>
<li><b>Quick Settings tile.</b> Shorten whatever is on your clipboard without opening the app.</li>
<li><b>Emoji aliases.</b> Pick from the server's emoji set with a built-in picker, rendered in Fluent 3D.</li>
<li><b>Link management.</b> Passwords, click limits, expiry, private stats, and bot blocking, all editable after the fact.</li>
<li><b>Analytics.</b> Clicks over time, plus breakdowns by country, browser, OS, and referrer, filterable and scoped per link.</li>
<li><b>Home-screen widgets.</b> Six chart styles, configured per instance, cached so they render stale data instead of spinners.</li>
<li><b>Styled QR codes.</b> Circle-module QR with the spoo mark in the middle, shareable or saved to your gallery.</li>
<li><b>Material 3 Expressive.</b> Motion is springs, not tweens. Dynamic color from your wallpaper or a seed of your choosing, across the app and its widgets. Dark mode everywhere, even the widgets.</li>
</ul>

You need a free spoo.me account. The app signs in through your browser, so there are no API keys to paste.

spoo.me is an open source URL shortener with advanced analytics. The whole stack, this app included, is AGPL and on GitHub.
